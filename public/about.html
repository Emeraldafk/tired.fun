Bonding Curve Dynamics

How the Bonding Curve Prices Tokens

When a user buys YES or NO tokens, the payment asset (ETH / Robinhood Chain native) is deposited directly into the smart contract vault. The contract computes token price using a deterministic power curve:

Price(S) = k × S2

As supply expands, each next token costs more. When exiting, the trader burns their tokens and withdraws the corresponding area under the curve instantly—guaranteeing continuous solvency with zero order book counterparty risk.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Traditional markets expire because they bet on a single fixed timestamp (e.g. "Will Candidate X win on Nov 5?"). Tired.Fun is built on cultural sentiment and perpetual conviction. Beliefs and opinions shift over weeks, months, and cycles. By letting markets run perpetually, traders can buy early conviction, take profit on hype surges, or hold long-term stances without arbitrary expiration dates.
When you purchase YES or NO tokens early in a market's lifecycle, you purchase along the lower end of the bonding curve. As more participants agree with your stance and buy tokens, the price moves higher along the curve. You can sell your tokens back to the smart contract vault at any time to lock in profits instantly.
Whenever a buy or sell transaction executes on either the YES or NO curve of your spawned market, a 1% protocol fee is deducted and sent directly to the creator's connected address on Robinhood Chain. Royalties are non-custodial and stream in real time.
